# AML and customer due diligence checklist

This records facts. It does not rate risk and it does not verify identity. A responsible
person does both and signs.

Requirements differ by jurisdiction and by professional body. Check yours. This is the common
core, not a compliance standard.

## Entity

- Legal name, trading name, company or registration number
- Registered address, and trading address where different
- Entity type and date of incorporation or formation
- Nature of the business, in plain words

## People

- Every director, partner or trustee: full name, date of birth, address
- Every beneficial owner over 25 per cent: name, percentage, how held
- Whether ownership is held through another entity, and if so the chain to the ultimate owner
- Who instructs the firm, and their authority to do so

**The step most often missed.** Beneficial ownership through a holding company. If the
shareholder is a company, the chain is not complete until a natural person is named.

## Checks recorded

- Identity evidence seen, type and date
- Address evidence seen, type and date
- Register or registry check, source and date
- Sanctions and PEP screening, source and date
- Source of funds, where the engagement requires it

## Risk

- Risk rating: assigned by a named person, with a date
- Reason for the rating, in one or two sentences
- Review date

## Output marking

Any field without an answer is written as `NOT PROVIDED`. Never leave a blank that reads as
"checked and nothing found", and never infer a value.
